Our Historic Ranch

Purcell Ranch blends Montana's rich heritage with breathtaking natural beauty to create unforgettable moments.

A Legacy of Hospitality

Established in the heart of Montana, Purcell Ranch has been welcoming guests for generations. Our venue combines the rustic charm of historic barn structures with the stunning backdrop of forest and meadow landscapes.

Located just 20 minutes from Missoula, our secluded yet accessible location offers the perfect balance of convenience and natural serenity for your special event.
